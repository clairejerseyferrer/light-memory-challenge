LMC scoring update — 2026-10-28

Requested rule: 100 points, minus 10 points for every whole second under 10 seconds.

Implemented formula for a successful round:
  secs = floor(response_time_ms / 1000)
  if secs >= 10: points = 100
  otherwise: points = max(0, 100 - 10 * (10 - secs))
  if response time exceeds the level deadline plus grace period: points = 0 and a life is lost.

Examples: 1s=10, 2s=20, ... 9s=90, 10s or more=100 (if within deadline).

Changed: firmware scoring threshold and comments; Excel formula workbook labels/parameter; documentation.
Unchanged: game flow, backend endpoints, dashboard, reconnection/failure handling, offline queue, retry handling, and payload structure.
Hardware and live-backend tests have not been performed by this script.
